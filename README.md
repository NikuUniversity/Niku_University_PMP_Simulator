## Program Overview
Welcome to the official 2026 curriculum repository for the **MBA in Project Management** at Niku University, Raj Empire campus, Kalyan East. This program is designed to bridge the gap between theoretical business strategy and real-world project execution. Tailored for modern industries—including IT, construction, infrastructure, manufacturing, and agile product development—our syllabus equips future leaders with advanced tools in risk mitigation, agile frameworks, financial modeling, and global stakeholder management.

---

## Vision & Learning Outcomes
* **Strategic Execution:** Align complex project portfolios with long-term organizational strategy.
* **Agile & Hybrid Mastery:** Seamlessly transition between predictive (Waterfall) and adaptive (Agile/Scrum/Kanban) methodologies.
* **Financial & Resource Acumen:** Master budgeting, cost-benefit analysis, earned value management (EVM), and resource leveling.
* **Leadership & Ethics:** Drive high-performing cross-functional teams with emotional intelligence, ethical governance, and conflict resolution skills.

## Complete Curriculum & Syllabus Design (2026)

### Semester 1: Foundations of Management & Project Initiation
*Focuses on core business principles and the foundational lifecycle of projects.*

* **MPM-101: Principles of Modern Management & Organizational Behavior**
  * Classical and modern management theories
  * Organizational culture, motivation, and leadership styles
  * Managing organizational change in dynamic business environments
* **MPM-102: Quantitative Techniques & Business Statistics**
  * Descriptive and inferential statistics for decision-making
  * Probability distributions and hypothesis testing
  * Decision trees and forecasting models
* **MPM-103: Financial Accounting & Managerial Economics**
  * Interpretation of balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements
  * Demand analysis, cost structures, and market pricing strategies
  * Capital budgeting principles
* **MPM-104: Fundamentals of Project Management & Integration**
  * The project life cycle and the PMBOK Guide framework
  * Developing the Project Charter and Preliminary Scope Statement
  * Project integration management and stakeholder identification

---

### Semester 2: Planning, Execution, & Technical Frameworks
*Deep dives into planning constraints, risk assessment, and technical execution tools.*

* **MPM-201: Project Scope, Time, & Cost Management**
  * Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) creation and dictionary development
  * Network diagrams, Critical Path Method (CPM), and PERT estimation
  * Cost estimating, budget baseline setting, and Earned Value Management (EVM)
* **MPM-202: Quality Assurance, Control, & Procurement Management**
  * Six Sigma, Total Quality Management (TQM), and ISO standards in projects
  * Procurement planning, contract types (FP, T&M, CR), and vendor selection
  * Supplier negotiation and contract administration
* **MPM-203: Agile, Scrum, & Hybrid Project Methodologies**
  * Manifesto for Agile Software Development
  * Scrum framework: Roles, artifacts, and ceremonies (Sprint Planning, Daily Standup, Retrospective)
  * Scaling Agile (SAFe) and blending predictive models with adaptive workflows
* **MPM-204: Business Communication & Professional Negotiation**
  * Technical writing for project documentation and reporting
  * Cross-cultural communication in global project environments
  * Principles of principled negotiation and conflict resolution

---

### Semester 3: Advanced Specializations & Digital Transformation
*Addresses enterprise-level challenges, risk management, and cutting-edge software.*

* **MPM-301: Enterprise Risk Management & Compliance**
  * Qualitative and quantitative risk analysis (Monte Carlo simulations)
  * Risk response planning, contingency reserves, and trigger monitoring
  * Legal, environmental, and regulatory compliance in project execution
* **MPM-302: Project Portfolio & Program Management (PfMP/PgMP aligned)**
  * Portfolio management vs. Program management vs. Project management
  * Strategic portfolio selection and resource optimization across multiple projects
  * Governance frameworks and Portfolio Balancing matrices
* **MPM-303: Digital Tools & Project Management Information Systems (PMIS)**
  * Hands-on training with Microsoft Project, Primavera P6, and Jira
  * Cloud-based collaboration tools (Asana, Notion, Trello)
  * Data analytics and dashboard creation (PowerBI/Tableau for PMs)
* **MPM-304: Elective Stream (Choose One):**
  * *Track A:* Infrastructure & Construction Project Management
  * *Track B:* IT & Software Product Development Management
  * *Track C:* Global Supply Chain & Operations Management

---

### Semester 4: Capstone, Industry Internship, & Global Leadership
*Practical application, leadership development, and independent research.*

* **MPM-401: Global Leadership, Governance, & Business Ethics**
  * Ethical dilemmas in project management and corporate social responsibility (CSR)
  * Managing distributed, multicultural, and remote teams
  * Sustainable project management practices (Green PM)
* **MPM-402: Comprehensive Industry Internship / Practicum**
  * 16-week immersive corporate attachment at partner firms across Mumbai/Kalyan/Thane industrial corridors
  * Real-time problem solving, workflow optimization, and project shadowing
* **MPM-403: Capstone Master’s Thesis / Live Project Implementation**
  * End-to-end execution, documentation, and defense of a live project proposal
  * Comprehensive project audit and post-implementation review report
